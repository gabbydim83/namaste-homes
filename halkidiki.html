Sea Breeze Paradise — Halkidiki

A secluded seaside escape made for slowing down.

Sea Breeze Paradise is a peaceful seaside stay in Halkidiki, surrounded by nature and beautiful views of the Aegean Sea.

Wake up to the sound of the waves and birds in the garden, step outside into the sea breeze and enjoy slow mornings, relaxed afternoons and memorable sunsets.

The accommodation itself is private, while the garden and barbecue area are shared.
