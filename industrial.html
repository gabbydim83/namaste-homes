Industrial Loft combines darker textures, black metal details, warm wood and clean architectural lines to create a confident urban atmosphere that still feels comfortable and welcoming.

The loft features a spacious, high-ceiling open-plan living and kitchen area on the lower level, together with a WC and a separate single bed with its own private room with a door. The other sleeping area is on the upper level with a comfortable double bed.

With its distinctive layout, private kitchen and comfortable living areas, Industrial Loft is ideal for up to three guests, whether travelling for a city break, a longer stay or enjoying Thessaloniki at your own pace.
