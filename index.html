Our story

About Namaste Homes

Since 2019, Namaste Homes has been creating distinctive stays in Thessaloniki and Halkidiki, where thoughtful design, comfort and genuine hospitality come together.

Inspired by the Indian greeting Namaste, a traditional expression of respect and welcome, we believe that every stay should feel warm, effortless and memorable. Our guests’ comfort, needs and experience are at the heart of everything we do, with attention to detail and a personal touch in every home.

From stylish city lofts to peaceful seaside escapes, discover thoughtfully designed spaces that invite you to relax, explore and feel at home.
