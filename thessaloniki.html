Namaste Lofts is a collection of three unique designer loft apartments in the eastern side of Thessaloniki, each created with its own distinctive character, atmosphere and story.

Located just a 5-minute walk from the Thessaloniki waterfront, and close to the city centre, White Tower, Aristotelous Square, Thessaloniki Exhibition Centre and major universities, Namaste Lofts offers a stylish and comfortable base for exploring the city, working, studying or simply enjoying a longer stay.

Each spacious, high-ceiling loft combines thoughtful design, modern comforts and carefully selected details, creating an experience that feels different from a conventional apartment stay.

01 · Choose Your Personality

Boho Loft

Inspired by the relaxed spirit of the Greek islands, with warm colours, textures and a laid-back atmosphere.

Warm, natural and effortlessly relaxed.

Boho Loft brings together soft neutral tones, natural textures, warm wood and woven details to create a calm urban retreat with the easy-going character of a Greek island stay.

The high-ceiling living area, private kitchen, elevated sleeping zone and carefully layered lighting give the loft an open, distinctive feel while keeping everything needed for a comfortable Thessaloniki stay close at hand.

For groups & families

Stay Together, Stay Your Way

Because all three lofts are located right next to each other, Namaste Lofts is particularly suited to families, groups of friends, couples travelling together, wedding and celebration groups, business groups and extended family stays.

Together, the three lofts can accommodate up to 9 guests, allowing larger groups to stay in the same location while still enjoying the privacy and comfort of their own individual apartment.

Whether you're visiting for a few nights, a few weeks or longer, each loft is fully equipped with the essentials for a comfortable stay, including Wi-Fi, air conditioning, private kitchens and thoughtfully designed living spaces.
