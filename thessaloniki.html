The collection

Three personalities. One address.

Namaste Lofts is a collection of three unique designer loft apartments in the eastern side of Thessaloniki, each created with its own distinctive character, atmosphere and story.

All three lofts are located next to each other on the ground floor, each with its own private entrance and a small balcony at the entrance, offering easy access and added privacy.

Located just a short walk from the waterfront and within easy reach of the city centre, the three lofts offer a stylish and comfortable base for a city break, a longer stay or a trip with friends and family.
